# Part 12A golden cases

## Fixture rules

Use the Phase 4 synthetic identities and fixed UUIDs. Set the trusted business date to 2026-09-24
and the application time zone to `Asia/Dubai`. Cases use synthetic rows only. `org_a` and `org_b` are
different tenants; `branch_a` and `branch_b` belong to `org_a`. Administrator A has selected
`branch_a`. Manager A has one current direct report. Employee A is linked to that report.

Generated byte assertions pin the renderer version, source JSON, fonts, logo digest, locale, line
endings, archive settings, and source timestamp. Record expected SHA-256 values when the renderer is
implemented. 12A fixes behavior and inputs; later parts supply executable byte fixtures.

## Completion use

Parts 12B through 12G turn their assigned cases into focused unit, HTTP, database, and browser
checks. Part 12H traces all 50 IDs exactly once and adds cross-part denial, restart, log-safety, and
reverse-rollback proof. A case can gain more assertions, but its authority, source owner, and
expected result cannot change without updating this contract and recording the reason.
